Hello,

we are the team behind spin.clinic and przeszłość.today (Poland). We collect Polish public data from official sources every day: the Sejm and Senate APIs, the court company register (KRS), public procurement (BZP, TED), election results (PKW) and the Polish sanctions lists of MSWiA and GIIF.

Your Poland page lists 5 Polish sources and about 3,700 political office-holders. We think we can help fill the gaps with sources that have a clear legal basis and public-interest value:
- ministers, deputy ministers and government plenipotentiaries (official government pages),
- voivodes and mayors of cities (PKW results, official pages),
- members of the Monetary Policy Council and the NBP board, judges of the Constitutional Tribunal, heads of the Supreme Audit Office and state agencies,
- management and supervisory boards of State Treasury companies (KRS),
- links to official asset declarations where they are published.

What we offer:
- crawlers written to your contribution guidelines (zavod), maintained by us, submitted through GitHub;
- corrections and identifiers (Sejm IDs, Wikidata) for the existing pl_sejm and pl_senate datasets;
- attribution of OpenSanctions on our pages and a link back to every entity.

What we would ask in return:
- a licence (or exemption) that lets us display OpenSanctions matches on spin.clinic and in przeszłość.today, including its paid tier, with attribution;
- recognition as a data contributor, if you have such an arrangement.

We only process data of public figures from official sources and follow GDPR. Could we have a 30-minute call in the coming weeks?

Kind regards,
The spin.clinic team
